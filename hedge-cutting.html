Professional hedge care

Clean lines,
healthy growth

A well-cut hedge does more than make a garden look tidy. It protects privacy, keeps paths and driveways usable, prevents growth pressing against buildings and encourages a dense, balanced structure.

We handle routine maintenance, formal shaping, substantial height and width reductions, and the careful restoration of hedges that have become too large for their setting. Laurel, privet, yew, conifer, beech, hornbeam and mixed native boundaries all respond differently, so the method is matched to the species rather than treating every hedge the same.

Before cutting, we check access, neighbouring boundaries and active nesting birds. Afterwards, paths, lawns and borders are cleared properly and the agreed green waste is removed.

Faq

Hedge Cutting,
questions answered

Straight answers about timing, reductions, access, clearance and quotations.

How often should a hedge be cut?

Most formal hedges benefit from one or two cuts during the growing season, while vigorous species may need more frequent attention. We recommend timing based on the species, condition and finish you want.

Can you reduce the height of an overgrown hedge?

Yes. The safe amount depends on the species and how much live growth remains. We inspect it first and will explain if a staged reduction is more likely to leave a healthy, attractive result.

Do you remove all hedge cuttings?

Yes. Unless you ask to keep the material, clippings and woody arisings are cleared from paths, lawns and borders and removed from site as part of the agreed job.

When is the best time to cut a hedge?

Light maintenance is often carried out during the growing season, but major work may suit another time depending on the species and wildlife. We check for active nesting birds before starting and postpone work where required.

Can you cut tall or difficult-access hedges?

Yes. We assess height, access, ground conditions and nearby property so the correct equipment and safe working method can be planned before the job.

How much does hedge cutting cost?

Cost depends on length, height, width, access, the amount being removed and disposal volume. We inspect the hedge and provide a clear, no-obligation written quote before anything is booked.
