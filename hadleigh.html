Tree surgery in Hadleigh

A wool town
shaped by the Brett

Hadleigh's prosperity as a medieval wool town survives in an extraordinary concentration of historic buildings. The long High Street runs parallel to the River Brett, while St Mary's Church, the Guildhall and Deanery Tower form a nationally important group near the churchyard. Trees here contribute to views, enclosed gardens and the setting of centuries-old fabric.

The riverside is different again. Between Bridge Street, Toppesfield Gardens and the River Walk, alder, willow and other moisture-loving trees sit on low ground beside paths, wet woodland and historic boundaries. Ground conditions, access and habitat can matter as much as crown shape, especially after prolonged rain.

Hadleigh Conservation Area reaches across much of this historic and riverside setting, and parts of the corridor carry TPO protection. Further east, the town climbs onto higher ground through later neighbourhoods with established garden trees and exposed boundaries. We identify the setting first, then specify work that suits it.

Working in Hadleigh

What changes
from street to river

Hadleigh's historic spine and low riverside ground demand different working methods. We account for:

  • Conservation-area controls, TPO groups and trees protected through planning conditions

  • Long historic plots behind the High Street with narrow passages and shared boundaries

  • Alder, willow and mature specimens on softer Brett-side ground beside paths and wet woodland

  • Trees affecting views or the setting of listed buildings, old walls and the churchyard group

Faq

Tree surgery in Hadleigh,
questions answered

Straight answers about protected trees, restricted access, nearby villages, emergencies and pricing in Hadleigh.

Can you work on protected trees in Hadleigh Conservation Area?

Yes. Hadleigh Conservation Area covers much of the historic core and River Brett setting, while some trees also have individual or group TPO protection. We check the constraints and prepare the correct specification before notice or consent is sought from Babergh District Council.

Can you work in narrow gardens behind Hadleigh High Street?

Yes. Where access is restricted by historic buildings, narrow passages or neighbouring boundaries, we can dismantle trees in controlled sections and plan how all timber and brash will leave the site.

Do you cover the villages around Hadleigh too?

Yes. We cover Layham, Kersey, Aldham, Raydon, Hintlesham, Whatfield, Elmsett and other villages around Hadleigh and the Brett Valley. Call if you would like us to confirm your address.

Will you need to check for a Tree Preservation Order (TPO)?

We check individual and group TPOs, conservation-area status and planning conditions before protected work. The correct route depends on the type of protection and the proposed work.

Do you offer emergency tree work in Hadleigh?

Yes. We provide 24/7 storm and emergency response for fallen trees, hanging branches and sudden hazards across Hadleigh and nearby villages. Keep people clear and tell us if a road, roof, river path or utility is affected.

How much does tree surgery cost in Hadleigh?

Cost depends on tree size, access, nearby historic structures, work specification, rigging and waste removal. We inspect the property free of charge and provide a written, no-obligation quote.
