Tree surgery in Castle Hedingham

A historic village
needs a careful approach

Castle Hedingham is not a generic suburban patch. The Norman castle sits above a village shaped by medieval routes and long-established property lines, with mature gardens running around Bayley Street, Castle Lane, Crown Street and Church Ponds. Those surroundings make proportion, access and the effect on neighbouring buildings especially important when tree work is planned.

The village has been a designated conservation area since 1969. That does not mean trees can never be pruned or removed, but it does mean protected work needs the right checks and, where applicable, notice to Braintree District Council. We establish whether a TPO or conservation-area controls apply before the first cut and help keep the process straightforward.

Outside the historic core, larger gardens, field edges and exposed rural boundaries bring a different set of jobs: crown management, deadwood over drives, storm damage and old stumps blocking the next use of the ground. Every quote begins with a site visit so the method fits the tree and the property.

Faq

Tree surgery in Castle Hedingham,
questions answered

Straight answers on protected trees, access, neighbouring property and quotes in Castle Hedingham.

Do you cover tree work within Castle Hedingham's conservation area?

Yes. Castle Hedingham has a designated conservation area, so qualifying tree work normally needs advance notice to Braintree District Council even where there is no individual TPO. We check the constraints before work and can guide you through the application process.

Can you work in a Castle Hedingham garden with narrow access?

Yes. We assess gateways, side passages, overhead lines, neighbouring property and drop zones during the free visit. Where access is restricted, we can dismantle a tree in controlled sections and plan how timber and brash leave the site.

Do you cover the villages around Castle Hedingham too?

Yes. We cover Sible Hedingham, Great Yeldham, Little Yeldham, Gestingthorpe, Toppesfield, Pebmarsh and other nearby villages. If your village is not listed, call and we will confirm.

Will you need to check for a Tree Preservation Order (TPO)?

We check for TPO and conservation-area constraints before protected work starts. A tree can have an individual TPO whether it sits inside or outside the conservation area, so location alone is not enough to decide.

Do you offer emergency tree work in Castle Hedingham?

Yes — we provide 24/7 storm and emergency response across Castle Hedingham and the surrounding area, for fallen or dangerous trees after high winds or storm damage.

How much does tree surgery cost in Castle Hedingham?

Price depends on the species and size, the work required, access, nearby structures, waste removal and whether specialist rigging is needed. We inspect the site free of charge and provide a written, no-obligation quote.
