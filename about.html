How we think

Cut less.
Plan more.

A chainsaw is only useful after the right decision has been made. Our approach is built around five habits:

  • Start with the problem you need solved, not an assumption that the whole tree must go

  • Check access, targets, neighbours, utilities and protected-tree constraints before pricing

  • Explain the proposed work in plain English and put the agreed scope in writing

  • Use ropes, rigging and controlled sections where gravity alone is not a safe plan

  • Finish with the site cleared, the details honoured and no mystery pile left behind

Faq

About the team,
questions answered

The useful things to know before inviting Limb Reaper to look at your trees.

Who runs Limb Reaper Tree Surgery?

Limb Reaper is run by Ryan. He carries out site visits, prepares written quotes and works on site alongside the team, so customers deal directly with someone who understands the job from first look to final clean-up.

Are Limb Reaper's climbers qualified and insured?

Yes. Climbers hold the relevant NPTC qualifications for the equipment and work they carry out, and Limb Reaper holds £10 million public liability insurance. Certificates can be shown before work starts.

Where does Limb Reaper work?

Limb Reaper is based near Sudbury and covers North Essex and Suffolk, including Halstead, Castle Hedingham, Clare, Colchester, Bury St Edmunds, Haverhill, Stowmarket, Hadleigh and the villages between them.

What tree services does Limb Reaper provide?

Services include tree felling and removal, crown reduction and thinning, pollarding, dead-wooding and pruning, stump grinding, crown lifting, site clearance and 24/7 emergency tree work.

Will you check whether a tree is protected?

Yes. Tree Preservation Orders, conservation-area controls and relevant planning conditions are checked before protected work starts, with guidance on the appropriate council process where needed.

How do Limb Reaper quotes work?

Every job begins with a free, no-obligation site visit. Access, tree condition, the proposed work and waste options are assessed before a clear written price is provided.
